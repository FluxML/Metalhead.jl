Internals

Nothing on this page is public API: none of it is exported or declared public, and it may change without notice, in any release, as timm, transformers and diffusers do. Using the models doesn't need it. The CLIP, BERT and sentence-transformers pages have their own internals, and the Porting page documents how a library registers its architectures.

Builders

create_model calls these with the architecture's default arguments, merged with the checkpoint's configuration and your keyword arguments. The timm builders take timm's keyword arguments, the transformers builders the fields of the model's config.json, and the diffusers builders the arguments of the model's constructor.

Metalhead.Transformers.gemma3_for_causal_lm — Method
gemma3_for_causal_lm(; vocab_size = 262208, hidden_size = 2304, num_hidden_layers = 26, kwargs...)

Port of transformers' Gemma3ForCausalLM, the Gemma 3 text model, built from the fields of its Gemma3TextConfig with the same names and defaults, and rope_theta, rope_local_base_freq, rope_scaling and sliding_window_pattern, which older config.jsons have in place of rope_parameters and layer_types. It takes and returns the arrays of llama_for_causal_lm.

Gemma 3 differs from Llama in its decoder layers: the token embeddings are multiplied by √hidden_size, the RMSNorms scale by 1 + weight, the outputs of the attention and of the MLP are normalised too, the queries and keys are normalised per head and scaled by query_pre_attn_scalar^-0.5, the MLP gate is gelu_pytorch_tanh, and the logits may be softcapped (final_logit_softcapping). The layers attend either to all the previous tokens ("full_attention") or to the last sliding_window of them ("sliding_attention"), as layer_types says, with rotary embeddings of their own: rope_parameters has one for each layer type. Bidirectional attention (use_bidirectional_attention) errors. attn_logit_softcapping is ignored, as transformers ignores it.

source
Metalhead.Transformers.granite_for_causal_lm — Method
granite_for_causal_lm(; vocab_size = 32000, hidden_size = 4096, num_hidden_layers = 32, kwargs...)

Port of transformers' GraniteForCausalLM (IBM Granite 3 and 4.1), built from the fields of its GraniteConfig with the same names and defaults, rope_theta and rope_scaling, which older config.jsons have in place of rope_parameters, and head_dim, which the attention reads where a config.json has it. It takes and returns the arrays of llama_for_causal_lm.

Granite is Llama with four multipliers: the token embeddings are multiplied by embedding_multiplier, the attention scores by attention_multiplier (in place of 1/√head_dim), the outputs of the attention and of the MLP by residual_multiplier, and the logits are divided by logits_scaling.

Metalhead.Transformers.granitemoe_for_causal_lm — Method

Port of transformers' GraniteMoeForCausalLM (IBM Granite 3 MoE), built from the fields of its GraniteMoeConfig with the same names and defaults, and the keys of granite_for_causal_lm that it adds (rope_theta, rope_scaling, head_dim). It takes and returns the arrays of llama_for_causal_lm.

Metalhead.Transformers.llama_for_causal_lm — Method
llama_for_causal_lm(; vocab_size = 32000, hidden_size = 4096, num_hidden_layers = 32, kwargs...)

Port of transformers' LlamaForCausalLM, built from the fields of its LlamaConfig (the config.json of a checkpoint), with the same names and defaults. create_model reads the generation settings of the checkpoint into its generation_config (see GenerationConfig), and ignores the other keys of config.json, as transformers does. The rotary embedding is the default one or, with rope_scaling or rope_parameters, one with rope_type = "linear" or "llama3" (Llama 3.1 and later); other types error.

The model maps token indices of size (seq_len, batch_size), or a vector for a single sequence, to logits of size (vocab_size, seq_len, batch_size), with causal attention. Token indices start at 1: add 1 to the ids of a Hugging Face tokenizer. A batch of sequences of different lengths, padded on the right, takes the tokenizer's attention_mask of size (seq_len, batch_size) as a keyword argument, which keeps the padding out of the keys. The output of its processor, a TextProcessor, can replace both: model(tok(texts)). forward_features returns the hidden states after the final norm, and forward_intermediates those of each layer.

With a KVCache, model(input_ids; cache) takes the new tokens of each sequence only and returns (logits, cache).

source

Metalhead.Transformers.qwen3_5_for_conditional_generation — Method
qwen3_5_for_conditional_generation(; text_config, vision_config, image_token_id = 248056,
                                   tie_word_embeddings = false)

Port of transformers' Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration, the vision-language model of the Qwen3.5 checkpoints, built from its Qwen3_5Config: the vision encoder qwen3_5_vision_model of vision_config, the decoder qwen3_5_text_model of text_config (both NamedTuples of their config's fields; other keys are ignored) and the output projection lm_head, tied to the token embeddings with tie_word_embeddings. Videos and the multi-token prediction weights (mtp.*) of the checkpoints are not supported.

The model maps token indices of size (seq_len, batch_size), or a vector, to logits of size (vocab_size, seq_len, batch_size), as llama_for_causal_lm does, and takes the keyword arguments attention_mask and, for images, pixel_values and image_grid_thw, as made by the image processor of a checkpoint (Qwen2VLImageProcessor). The token indices then hold, for each image in order, a run of prod(image_grid_thw[:, i]) ÷ 4 image tokens (image_token_id + 1, <|image_pad|>), between the tokens of <|vision_start|> and <|vision_end|>, as the chat template of the checkpoints writes them. The merged tokens of the image replace the image tokens, and the multimodal rotary embedding gives them the positions of their grid. The processor of a checkpoint, a Qwen3VLProcessor, makes all of these from images and a prompt with a placeholder for each image, and the model takes its output as it is: model(proc(; images, text)).

Metalhead.Transformers.qwen3_5_moe_text_model — Method
qwen3_5_moe_text_model(; vocab_size = 248320, hidden_size = 2048, num_hidden_layers = 40, kwargs...)

Port of transformers' Qwen3_5MoeTextModel, the decoder of Qwen3.5 MoE, built from the fields of its Qwen3_5MoeTextConfig with the same names and defaults, and full_attention_interval, rope_theta, rope_scaling and partial_rotary_factor, which the config reads from config.json. Metalhead.Transformers.qwen3_5_text_model — Method
qwen3_5_text_model(; vocab_size = 248320, hidden_size = 4096, num_hidden_layers = 32, kwargs...)

Port of transformers' Qwen3_5TextModel, the decoder of Qwen3.5, built from the fields of its Qwen3_5TextConfig with the same names and defaults, and full_attention_interval, rope_theta, rope_scaling and partial_rotary_factor, which the config reads from config.json. It maps token indices of size (seq_len, batch_size), or a vector, to the hidden states after the final norm, and takes the keywords attention_mask and position_ids (see qwen3_5_decode, which takes input embeddings instead).

Its layers have either a gated delta rule ("linear_attention", Qwen3_5GatedDeltaNet) or causal attention ("full_attention"), as layer_types says, by default every full_attention_interval-th layer. The attention has a sigmoid gate on its output, norms its queries and keys per head and rotates the first head_dim * partial_rotary_factor features of each with the multimodal rotary embedding of rope_parameters["mrope_section"] (only the default rope_type). The RMSNorms scale by 1 + weight. The output of the gated delta rule is gated with SiLU, as in transformers, which ignores output_gate_type: it may be nothing, "silu" or "swish" (Qwen3.8's), and other values are an error.

Metalhead.Transformers.qwen3_5_vision_model — Method
qwen3_5_vision_model(; depth = 27, hidden_size = 1152, num_heads = 16, patch_size = 16,
                     spatial_merge_size = 2, temporal_patch_size = 2, kwargs...)

Port of transformers' Qwen3_5VisionModel, the vision encoder of Qwen3.5, built from the fields of its Qwen3_5VisionConfig (the vision_config of a Qwen3.5 checkpoint) with the same names and defaults. deepstack_visual_indexes, which config.json may carry, must be empty, as transformers' Qwen3.5 has no deepstack mergers; the rotary embedding is the axial one (rope_type = "axial", or "default").

The model takes (pixel_values, grid_thw), as the image processor of a checkpoint (Qwen2VLImageProcessor) makes them, whose output it also takes as it is (model(ip(images))), and returns the NamedTuple (; last_hidden_state, pooler_output):

  • pixel_values, of size (in_channels * temporal_patch_size * patch_size^2, num_patches), is the transpose of transformers' pixel_values: each column is a patch, flattened as torch's (in_channels, temporal_patch_size, patch_size, patch_size) (that is, as a Julia array of size (patch_size, patch_size, temporal_patch_size, in_channels), width first).
  • grid_thw, an integer matrix of size (3, num_images) (transformers' grid_thw transposed), has a column (t, h, w) for each image or video: its number of patches along time (frames of temporal_patch_size), height and width. The patches of the images follow one another; within an image, frame after frame, each frame is ordered by windows of spatial_merge_size × spatial_merge_size patches, row-major over the windows and row-major within each window.
  • last_hidden_state has size (hidden_size, num_patches). pooler_output, the merged tokens that the language model takes, image after image, has size (out_hidden_size, num_patches ÷ spatial_merge_size^2).

Each frame of each image attends to its own patches only.

Metalhead.Transformers.chunk_gated_delta_rule — Function
chunk_gated_delta_rule(q, k, v, g, beta; chunk_size = 64)

transformers' torch_chunk_gated_delta_rule with use_qk_l2norm_in_kernel = true, for the queries and keys q and k (headkdim, heads, seqlen, batch), the values v (headvdim, heads, seqlen, batch), the log decays g and the rates beta (heads, seqlen, batch). For each head, with q and k normalised and q scaled by `headkdim^-0.5, the stateS` (headvdim, headk_dim) starts at zero and at each token becomes S = exp(g) * S then S += beta * (v - S * k) * kᵀ, and the output is S * q.

As in transformers, the sequence is padded to chunks of chunk_size tokens, computed in parallel but for the state carried from one chunk to the next, in at least Float32. Each chunk solves a unit triangular system, here by inverting its matrix by blocks, which runs on a GPU and is differentiable. The output has the element type of v.

Metalhead.Transformers.moe_experts — Function
moe_experts(gate_up, down, act, x, top_k_index, top_k_weights)

The experts of a sparse mixture of experts, as transformers' MixtralExperts: each token, a column of x (hidden, n), goes through its experts top_k_index (k, n), whose outputs down * (act(gate) .* up) are summed with the weights top_k_weights (k, n). The stacked weights of the experts are torch's gate_up_proj (numexperts, 2 * intermediate, hidden) and `downproj(num_experts, hidden, intermediate), sogateup` has size (hidden, 2 * intermediate, numexperts) and down (intermediate, hidden, num_experts).
